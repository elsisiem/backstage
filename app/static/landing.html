The live layer for everyone who is not in the room.

A conference has eight rooms and you are in one. It has ten thousand people who care and a thousand who came. Backstage reads the public internet as the day happens, resolves every post to the session it is about, and turns the result into a graph you can walk into: which room is loud right now, what just shipped that touches your repo, the minute you missed.

Why this exists

Hybrid is dying, visas are tightening, and recordings land weeks later. The people outside the room are the majority, and they get nothing live.

Organizers are cutting remote access because livestreaming is cost-prohibitive. Research conferences are opening second sites because speakers cannot get visas. Inside the venue the problem is the same in miniature: the number one piece of feedback at the largest cloud-native conference was the schedule builder, because every attendee is outside seven of eight rooms at any moment.

“Costs for supporting livestreaming at the conference venue have simply become cost-prohibitive.”CSCW 2025 organizers, on cutting remote attendance
“Further, we'll pretend that they're not all going to be on YouTube the following week.”Corey Quinn, re:Invent justification letter

How it works

The public internet, resolved to the agenda, into a graph with reasons on every edge.

Four agents share one FalkorDB graph. The Listener ingests posts and recovers exact timestamps from platform post IDs. The Resolver decides where each post belongs and writes the signals it used onto the edge. The Wire parses announcements as they ship and tags the parts of a stack they touch. The Curator turns spikes into Moments. Every answer in the console is a path you can read.

What you get

[MOMENTS]

The minute you would have walked over for

A spike of reactions in one room, an announcement starting to land, the whole venue posting at once. Each Moment carries its evidence and the time, so you can walk over now or jump to those minutes in the recording later.

[WIRE]

Announcements as actions for your repo

Every changelog entry and launch post is parsed and tagged with the parts of a stack it touches. Paste a repository and the graph answers which of today's announcements change something you depend on, and which sessions are yours.

[FROM THE ROOM]

One line back from inside

Anyone in a room can send a single line, no account needed. It is resolved and attributed like any post and reaches every open console the moment it lands. That is what makes the layer two-sided.
