      DEMO GUIDE

      six steps, under three minutes. Each one runs from here, or
      1. STEP 01Start the keynote replay.

        Day 1 of GitHub Universe 2025 at 08:55, two hours per minute. The rooms, the clock and the counters are the real day, recovered from post IDs.

      2. STEP 02Read the venue, not a room.

        The card at the top summarizes every room at once: who is loud, what shipped, what you missed. Then look at one room.

      3. STEP 03Ask the graph why.

        Click a post under the keynote. The placement is a path: author is the speaker, posted minutes into the slot, reacting to an announcement made on that stage.

      4. STEP 04Build your Backstage.

        A repo's manifests become facets. Repo to facet to announcement, repo to facet to session: two hops, with evidence.

      5. STEP 05Ask in plain words.

        Which speakers posted about their own session, what overlapped with the loudest talk, which sessions were live only. Templated and free-form questions compile to read-only Cypher.

      6. STEP 06Report from the room.

        One line from inside a room reaches everyone outside it, resolved and attributed, live. That is what makes the layer two-sided.
